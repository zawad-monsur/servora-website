Getting started

What do I need to run ClochePOS?

Any laptop, PC or Android tablet with a modern browser. For receipts, any 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer works. Nothing to install except an optional small helper for silent printing on Windows.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days with everything in your plan switched on. No card needed. You get your own address, like yourname.clochepos.com, and a sample menu to try things out.

Will you set it up for us?

Yes. Send a photo of your menu and we enter dishes, sizes, add-ons and Bangla names, then train your team on-site or on video.

Does it work for sit-down restaurants with many tables?

Yes. You draw your own floor plan, see which tables are free or seated, and orders go straight to the kitchen screen. Guests can move tables, tables can be merged, and bills split.

Daily use

What happens if the internet goes down?

The register keeps selling. Paid orders are saved on the device and sync with their real time and cash drawer when the connection is back, with no double sales. The kitchen screen and QR menu need the connection to update.

How does it stop cash going missing?

Each shift starts with opening cash. At closing the cashier counts without seeing the expected amount, and the system shows exactly what is short or over. Every discount and cancelled bill is recorded with who did it and why.

Do you support VAT and Mushak?

VAT is built in, inclusive or exclusive, with your BIN on every receipt. Mushak 6.3 invoices and VAT reports are coming.

Which AI features are available?

AI is coming soon: questions about your business in Bangla, theft and loss alerts, a smart menu for guests and menu set-up from a photo. They will be add-ons; nothing to change.

Billing & data

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Modules switch on in seconds and your data stays where it is.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Pay monthly, or yearly to get two months free. You can pay by bKash, bank transfer or cash.

What happens if a payment is late?

You get a reminder and a few days’ grace. After that the system goes read-only: you can still see and export everything, just not take new sales, until the payment is in.

Is my data safe? Who owns it?

Your data is yours. Each restaurant has its own separate database, backed up daily, and you can export your sales, menu and customers at any time. See our privacy policy.
