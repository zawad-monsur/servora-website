Tables & kitchen

Your floor and kitchen, talking to each other.

Live table floor plan

  • Draw your own floors: tables, counter, doors and walls
  • Free, seated or waiting too long, with time and bill on every table
  • Move guests, merge two tables or split one bill

Kitchen screen with timers

  • Orders appear the moment they are placed, with timers that turn red when late
  • Only new items go to the kitchen when a table orders more
  • Separate screens and printers for the kitchen and the bar
  • Guests see received, preparing and ready on their phone

Cash that adds up

Know exactly what should be in the drawer.

  • Every shift starts with opening cash and records every taka in or out
  • A blind count at closing shows exactly what is short or over
  • Every discount and cancelled bill is recorded with who and why
  • VAT plus service charge, packaging fee or SD
