What we do

One system for the whole restaurant.

Servora runs the day-to-day of restaurants, cafés and food businesses in one place: orders and payments, tables and the kitchen, the cash drawer, stock and food cost, customers and the numbers. We set it up for you, enter your menu, train your team and stay with you on WhatsApp, so you can focus on food and guests while the system keeps count.

First, what is a POS?

POS means point of sale: the system at your counter where orders are taken and bills are paid. A basic POS only prints the bill. Servora connects that bill to your tables, kitchen, cash drawer, stock and reports, so every order updates everything else on its own.

Cash that adds up

Know exactly how much should be in the drawer.

Most restaurants lose money quietly: a missing note at closing, a discount nobody approved, stock that disappears. Servora closes those gaps.

  • Every shift starts with the opening cash and records every taka taken out or added
  • At closing the cashier counts without seeing the expected amount, then sees exactly how much is short or over
  • An end-of-shift report for each cashier: sales, payment methods, discounts and cancellations
  • Every discount and cancelled bill is recorded with who did it and why
  • VAT plus any extra charges you need: service charge, packaging fee or SD

FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What do I need to run Servora?

Any laptop, PC or Android tablet with a modern browser and an internet connection. For receipts, any 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer works.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Today Servora needs an internet connection. An offline register that keeps selling and syncs later is on our roadmap and is one of our top priorities.

Does it work for sit-down restaurants with many tables?

Yes. You draw your own floor plan, staff see which tables are free or seated, and orders go straight to a kitchen screen. Guests can be moved to another table, two tables merged, or a bill split in two.

How does it stop cash going missing?

Each shift starts with the opening cash. At closing the cashier counts without seeing the expected amount, and the system shows exactly how much is short or over. Every discount and cancelled bill is recorded with who did it and why.

Which AI features are available?

AI is coming soon: asking your business questions in Bangla, theft and loss alerts, a smart menu that suggests dishes to guests, menu set-up from a photo and prep forecasts. Clients get them as add-ons without changing systems.

Is my data safe? Who owns it?

Your data is yours. It is backed up daily, and you can export your sales, menu and customers at any time.

Do you support VAT and Mushak?

VAT is built in today, inclusive or exclusive, with your BIN on every receipt. Mushak 6.3 invoices and monthly VAT reports are coming next.

Can I start with one plan and upgrade later?

Yes. Modules switch on instantly and your data stays exactly where it is.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Pay monthly, or yearly to get two months free.
