Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information ClochePOS (“we”) keeps, why, and what you can do about it. It covers our website and the ClochePOS service.

What we keep

  • Account details: your name, restaurant name, phone, email and the address you choose (like yourname.clochepos.com).
  • Your business data: menu, orders, payments, stock, staff accounts and anything else you enter. This belongs to you.
  • Your guests’ data: if you record customers or collect feedback, their name, phone, visits and comments. You decide what to collect; we keep it on your behalf.
  • Billing records: your plan, invoices and payments to us.
  • Technical logs: IP addresses, browser type and error reports, kept for security and to fix problems.

How we use it

Only to run the service for you: sign you in, show your data on your screens, send invoices and reminders, help with support and keep the system secure. We do not sell your data or your guests’ data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

Each restaurant has its own separate database on our servers, backed up daily. Access is limited to the people who run the service, and only when needed for support or maintenance.

Who else sees it

We share data only with providers we need to run the service (such as our hosting company and message delivery), under confidentiality, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. After you cancel we keep it for 60 days so you can come back or take a copy, then delete it. Backups roll over within 30 days after that. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your choices

  • Export your data at any time from the app.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your account details.
  • If a guest asks you to delete their details, you can do it in the app, or ask us to help.

Our website

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. The contact buttons open WhatsApp or your email app; nothing is sent until you press send there.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email us or message us on WhatsApp.
